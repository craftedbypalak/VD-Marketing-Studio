# YouTube long-form case studies for Venture Drafters: research note

Date: 29 October 2026. Builds on `Marketing/_research/content-strategy-2026-10/research-whatsapp-youtube.md`, Part 2 (audience facts, B2B trust, the "video library for sales" reframe). Those conclusions stand; this note adds newer evidence and converts it into rules.

Format under review: one 30 to 60 minute case study a month on one real internal system, in the order problem, cost, design idea, build on screen, what changed, how to start; scripted voiceover, b-roll, on-screen text, a few founder-on-camera clips; Reels re-uploaded as Shorts.

Labels: [E] sourced (URL, date); [E, secondary] non-official source reporting an official figure; [E, weak] vendor or practitioner claim; [I] inference.

## 1. Does 30 to 60 minute B2B walkthrough content hold an audience?

- Share of YouTube viewing time on videos of 20 minutes or more rose from 63.6% (January 2024) to 67.7% (December 2025); TV-set viewing rose from 28.2% to 33.7%. Eighteen countries measured; India is not among them. [E] Digital i, "The YouTube Era", June 2026, https://www.audiovisual451.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Digital-i-June-2026-The-YouTube-Era-Public-Version.pdf
- After YouTube's January 2025 recommendation change, the average length of algorithm-suggested videos fell from about 80 to about 40 minutes, and of the most popular videos from about 35 to about 28 minutes (Little Dot Studios, 800+ channels). Very long videos are no longer favoured by default. [E, secondary] Tubefilter, 12 February 2026, https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/12/little-dot-youtube-video-length-whitepaper-viewer-habits-2026/
- Retention benchmarks for 30 to 60 minute videos are unofficial and conflict (25 to 35% average percentage viewed "normal", 35 to 40% "strong"). [E, weak] https://prepublish.ai/blog/youtube-retention-benchmarks-2026 (2026). The reliable comparison is the channel's own "typical retention" line, drawn from its ten most recent videos of similar length. [E] YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
- Inference: 30 to 45 minutes is the safer target for a new channel; an hour must be earned by the build, not planned. [I]

**First 30 seconds.** Analytics reports an "Intro" metric, the share still watching after 30 seconds; a low figure means the opening did not match thumbnail and title. [E] Help 9314415. YouTube's own Shorts-to-long-form guide says the long video must pay off its promise "within its first 5 to 10 seconds, with no channel intro or unrelated updates first." [E] YouTube Blog, 14 July 2026, https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/ . For this format: open on the problem and the cost figure. [I]

**Pacing.** YouTube's Key moments guidance: if top moments fall late, move them earlier, "since audience size typically decreases over the video's length"; dips are skipped sections; spikes are rewatched sections, which can mean interest or confusion. [E] Help 9314415. The build on screen is the likely top moment; the result should be previewed early. [I]

**Face versus screen.** No rigorous 2025 to 2026 study compares voiceover-plus-screen with talking head for B2B retention or trust; vendor estimates (faceless channels 10 to 35% lower view duration) disagree with each other. [E, weak] https://virvid.ai/blog/faceless-youtube-algorithm-retention-2026 . Inference: the screen carries the proof, the face carries the trust; show the face at the problem, at the verdict and at the close. [I]

**Small B2B channels doing long walkthroughs well.**
- Arvow (founder channel, 28,100 subscribers, July 2026): 20 to 30 minute founder-led tutorials; one tutorial draws 65% of views from YouTube search; founder reports most customers come from YouTube. Bulk Mockup (9,350 subscribers): each video answers one problem raised in support tickets; a 370-view tutorial produced three customers. Scalelist (2,660 subscribers): screen-share workflow tutorials; the founder tracks which video precedes each sign-up, not subscribers. [E] SellOnTube, updated 22 October 2026, https://sellontube.com/blog/youtube-marketing-case-studies
- Jono Catliff and Nick Saraev (automation agencies, 100,000+ and 200,000+ subscribers): "blueprint-style" problem, workflow, build walkthroughs and hours-long free courses. [E, secondary] https://www.atakinteractive.com/blog/17-ai-youtubers-were-actually-watching-right-now (2026)
- Common pattern: the title is the literal task the buyer searches; one problem per video; the build is shown; a call to action early and mid-video. [E] SellOnTube, above. No Indian channel doing long system walkthroughs for MSME owners was found; Hindi business channels located are idea, franchise and coaching formats, with no length or retention data. [E] https://labourlawadvisor.in/blog/youtube-channels-for-business-owner-and-entrepreneurs/

## 2. Titles, thumbnails, descriptions, chapters, end screens, pinned comments

- Title limit 100 characters; description limit 5,000; YouTube advises that the first lines of the description are what viewers see before "Show more". [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404 ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/187594 ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449 . Third-party counts of the fold range from 100 to 170 characters; the "under 60" title rule has no official backing (a practitioner guide suggests under 70, keyword first). [E, weak] https://typecount.com/blog/youtube-description-character-limit ; https://alanspicer.com/youtube-metadata-optimization-title-description-tags-2026/
- Chapters: at least three timestamps, ascending, the first at 00:00, each at least 10 seconds; manual chapters override automatic ones. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
- Thumbnails: Test and Compare runs up to three variants and keeps the one with the highest watch-time share. [E] TechCrunch, 23 September 2026, https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/youtube-adds-new-creator-tools-like-video-a-b-testing-dynamic-thumbnails-and-live-dubbing/ . Faces reportedly help click-through only above about 200,000 subscribers. [E, secondary] https://fyreinteractive.co/blog/do-you-need-to-show-your-face-on-youtube
- End screens: video at least 25 seconds; last 5 to 20 seconds; up to four elements at 16:9; an external-website element requires Partner Program membership. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789 . Until then the website link goes in the description and pinned comment. [I]
- Pinned comments: no official guidance; practitioner consensus is one short pin with a specific next step, corrections and a few timestamps. [E, weak] https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/what-to-write-in-your-youtube-pinned-comment-to-get-a-reaction-from-your-audience
- Language: no 2025 to 2026 study compares English, Hindi and Hinglish YouTube titles. Agency opinion holds that English carries search intent and Hinglish carries engagement in social and WhatsApp copy; "business kaise shuru kare" style queries are described but not quantified. [E, weak] https://palvdm.com/blog/hinglish-content-seo/ ; https://contentserp.in/keyword-research-tool-india/ . The earlier note's rule stands: English title and description for search, a Hinglish phrase permitted in the title for local audiences, tested against the channel's own search report. [I]

## 3. Shorts from the long video

- Shorts may be up to 3 minutes. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070 . Related video link: in Studio, open the Short, choose "Related video", pick the long video. YouTube's advice: each clip must stand alone, with a spoken and visual pointer in its final five seconds. [E] YouTube Blog, 14 July 2026, above. Third-party publishing tools cannot set this link. [E] https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/13643189-can-i-add-a-link-to-my-long-form-youtube-video-when-publishing-shorts-via-vizard
- Do Shorts hurt long-form? YouTube's reported position is no evidence of harm, with recommendation working at video and topic level. [E, secondary] https://www.pandavideo.com/blog/shorts-and-long-form-videos-same-channel . A 2024 academic study of 250 creators found long-form views fell after Shorts. [E, secondary] https://ytgrowth.io/blog/shorts-vs-long-form . An 18,000-channel comparison (March 2025 to March 2026) found business and education channels keeping Shorts at 25 to 40% of uploads led long-only channels by 37 to 39% in subscribers, and advises staying under 55%; correlation only. [E] AIR Media-Tech, updated 23 October 2026, https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/should-you-chase-shorts-views-or-double-down-on-long-form-for-channel-growth
- How many: vendor guides cite 10 to 15 clips from a 30-minute video but also say to publish only clips that stand alone; best-retaining length cited is 20 to 45 seconds. [E, weak] https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/how-to-repurpose-long-form-content-shorts.html ; https://vidiq.com/blog/post/Clip-youtube-videos-long-to-short/ . For one person, two to four Shorts per case study, each re-hooked in its first line, keeps the Shorts share in band. [I]
- Posting times (IST): long-form weekdays 10:00 to 15:00; Shorts 18:00 to 21:00. Vendor studies, mostly global; the channel's own audience-activity report overrides them. [E, weak] https://nuwizo.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube/ ; https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube/ (2026)

## 4. Production for one person

- Frequency correlates with growth: under one upload a month gave 0.53% median monthly view growth versus 2.18% at 12+ (10.2 million channels). [E] vidIQ, July 2026, https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-upload-frequency-study/ . A separate study found fewer than two uploads a month underperformed, with little difference above that. [E] https://air.io/en/audience-growth/does-posting-more-on-youtube-help-we-studied-18000-channels-to-find-out . One long video plus its Shorts clears the floor; growth is not the objective. [I]
- Voiceover-plus-b-roll versus talking head: no measured evidence (Section 1). The scripted format suits a solo producer because it records in pieces and re-cuts; the three on-camera moments offset the "faceless" risk. [I]
- Captions: auto-captions are reported weakest on mixed Hindi-English speech; accuracy figures (55 to 95%) are vendor estimates. [E, weak] https://capsai.co/blog/how-to-generate-hindi-subtitles-automatically-for-youtube-videos-2025-guide . Generate, correct by hand, choose Roman script for Hinglish, upload the corrected file. [I]
- AI tools: auto-dubbing is open to all creators in 27 languages, with Expressive Speech in Hindi. [E] YouTube Blog, February 2026, https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-auto-dubbing-expressive-speech/ . Ask Studio gives draft feedback and title and thumbnail alternatives; Dynamic Thumbnails serve one of three per viewer; Shorts cut-testing arrives in 2027. [E] TechCrunch, 23 September 2026, above.
- AI labelling: disclosure is required for realistic altered or synthetic content (a real person made to say something, altered real footage, a realistic scene that did not occur). Not required: AI-written scripts, titles, thumbnails, caption creation, and "cloning one's own voice to create voice overs or dubs". A synthetic narrator imitating no one is not addressed. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

## 5. Measuring the video library for sales

- Unlisted videos: shareable by URL, usable in playlists and channel sections, commentable, absent from search and recommendations; anyone with the link can reshare. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/157177 ; https://www.gumlet.com/learn/youtube-unlisted-vs-private-video-hosting/
- Traffic sources: the "External" card lists sites and apps that embed or link the video; WhatsApp clicks may land in External or Direct/unknown depending on referrer. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314355 ; [E, weak] https://www.thumblore.com/blogs/youtube-traffic-sources-explained . Embed analytics are aggregate only. [E] https://www.cloudpresent.co/blog/embedding-a-video (2026)
- No benchmark exists for "watched before the call" or its effect on conversion. No evidence found. Practical measure: a CRM field per prospect (sent, confirmed watched), the video-level retention report (needs 100 views and 60 seconds) and the External card. [I] Scalelist's practice of tracking which video precedes each sign-up is the nearest precedent. [E] SellOnTube, above.

## 6. New in 2026 that changes the above

- Partner Program: entry remains 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views. [E] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 . From 1 February 2027 new applicants reportedly need 8,000 hours or 20 million Shorts views. [E, secondary] https://metricool.com/youtube-partner-program-2027-changes/ . Relevant only because end-screen website links stay unavailable. [I]
- Made on YouTube, 23 September 2026: Ask Studio on mobile with autonomous thumbnail refresh, Dynamic Thumbnails, Shorts cut tests (2027), live auto-dubbing pilot (2027), conversational AI editing in Shorts. [E] https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/youtube/made-on-youtube-updates-2026/
- Hindi audience: no official 2026 share figure found; third-party analyses repeat "about 500 million Indian users" and call Hindi the platform's search language. [E, weak] https://amlcommunications.com/hindi-hegemony-youtube-2026-global-india-analysis/ . YouTube's India report claims over Rs 18,000 crore of GDP in 2025, seen only second-hand. [E, secondary] https://www.threads.com/@youtube/post/Da3zk6Uj5R8/way-to-go
- The long-form and TV shift (Section 1) is measured outside India; the direction is plausible for India but unmeasured. [I]

|---|---|---|

| Face on camera | Three moments: problem, "what changed", close; the build stays on screen | [I] |
| Chapters | One per section (six minimum), first at 00:00, each at least 10 seconds, ascending | [E] Help 9884579 |
| Title | 100-character limit; keep under 70; task-first; English, optional Hinglish phrase | [E] Help 57404; [E, weak] Spicer 2026; [I] |
| Description | First 150 characters: system and outcome; then chapters; then website link; 5,000 limit | [E] Help 187594, 12948449; [E, weak] |
| Thumbnail | Three variants in Test and Compare; no reliance on a face | [E] TechCrunch 23 Sep 2026 |
| End screen | Last 5 to 20 seconds; up to four elements; next case study plus subscribe; no website element until YPP | [E] Help 6388789 |
| Pinned comment | One short next step plus corrections | [E, weak] AIR 2025 |
| Shorts per long video | Two to four, standalone, 20 to 45 seconds, re-hooked first line, Related video set in Studio | [E] YouTube Blog Jul 2026; [E, weak]; [I] |
| Shorts share of uploads | Below 55%; aim 25 to 40% | [E] AIR Media-Tech Oct 2026 |
| Posting time, IST | Long: Tuesday to Thursday 10:00 to 15:00; Shorts: 18:00 to 21:00; override with channel data | [E, weak] vendor studies 2026 |
