## 1. Tiered nurture: Dream 100, ABM and the evidence on cadence

**Dream 100 (Chet Holmes, *The Ultimate Sales Machine*, 2007).** A short list of the "best buyers" who "will spend more money, more quickly, and more often", pursued "again and again". Holmes's magazine case won 30 of 167 target advertisers in year one, 30 more in year two, the rest in year three. **[E]** Selling Power, 13 Jan 2010, https://www.sellingpower.com/2010/01/13/2167/the-list-that-will-change-your-life. A secondary summary describes a fixed rotating cadence for a year across mail, phone, email, a research piece, an invitation and an in-person touch, with results in "two to four quarters"; its "six touches a month" is the summariser's arithmetic. **[E, secondary]** https://pulserevops.com/knowledge/bs0016. No primary-source frequency found. **[gap]** Transferable principle: a named list, several channels, patience measured in quarters, touches that are mostly useful material rather than asks. **[I]**

**ABM for small firms.** Vendor guides agree on three tiers (one-to-one for 10 to 20 accounts, one-to-few clusters of 5 to 15, one-to-many for the rest), promotion between tiers by engagement, and "slow and high-quality" cadence at the top. No tested cadence for a one-person professional services firm was found. **[E, vendor]** https://www.mapsleads.co/blog/abm-tiering-1-2-3-explained

**Referral-network guidance.**
- BNI asks members to hold "at least one 1-2-1 per week" and to send a completed GAINS exchange (goals, accomplishments, interests, networks, skills) beforehand. **[E]** BNI, 24 May 2023, https://www.bni.com/the-latest/blog-news/1-2-1s-that-grow-your-business/. BNI's site is reported to say a one-to-one "at least once every 6 months" maintains an existing relationship. **[E, seen only in a search summary of bni.com]** Ivan Misner's VCP sequence (visibility, credibility, profitability) warns against "premature solicitation". **[E]** https://pod.wave.co/podcast/the-official-bni-podcast/episode-914-5-important-things-for-great-networking-4d3514b2
- Keith Ferrazzi's "pinging" (small personal contacts that say "you are important") is summarised as 3 to 10 a year per contact; no tier-by-tier frequency from Ferrazzi was found. **[E, secondary]** https://www.productivity501.com/the-art-of-pinging/9155
- Adam Grant's *Give and Take* rests on Levin, Walter and Murnighan (Organization Science, 2011): executives who reconnected with dormant ties got advice as valuable as from current ties, combining the novelty of weak ties with the trust of strong ones. **[E]** https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ororsc:v:22:y:2011:i:4:p:923-939. A 2024 follow-up finds reconnection works when both sides remember the tie, catch up, and see it similarly. **[E]** https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ororsc/v35y2024i2p573-600.html. A quiet Silver contact is therefore not lost; a candid "it has been a year" message is the evidence-backed move. **[I]**

**Studies on touch frequency.**
- Dagger, Danaher and Gibbs (Journal of Service Research, 2009; 591 service customers): contact frequency strengthens new relationships; in long relationships frequency has no effect and duration matters more. **[E, abstract only; full text paywalled]** https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1094670508331251
- Kitces Research (621 US advisers, 14 Jul 2025): median 14 client touchpoints a year at the lowest revenue tier, 20 at the highest; a "standardised high touch" mix (about 12 monthly newsletters plus a few calls and personal emails) had the highest median revenue per adviser; correlation only. **[E]** https://www.kitces.com/blog/financial-advisor-client-touchpoints-revenue-service-personalization-roi-cost-strategies/
- Hinge Research (2015, 523 firms): 81.5% of professional services firms have received a referral from someone who was never a client; "visible expertise" drove 37.3% of referrals, client relationships 23.1%, social relationships 17.7%. **[E, vendor research]** https://hingemarketing.com/blog/story/study-reveals-the-role-of-reputation-and-relationships-in-referral-marketin
- A law-firm column cites "roughly 12 touches a year" as consensus, without a study. **[E, weak]** https://natlawreview.com/article/how-to-stay-connected-referral-sources-when-your-life-crazy-busy
- **No study linking touch frequency to referral volume in professional services was found.** [gap]

**Synthesis [I].** Frequency matters most for new relationships (Platinum prospects, new introducers); established Gold relationships need regularity and reciprocity, not volume. Referrals come from people who know the firm's expertise, so some touches must carry evidence of it; that is the only reason own content belongs in the mix.

## 2. Touch types: what reads as care, what reads as promotion

**Festivals.** The Indore Collector's 2025 local holidays were Makar Sankranti (14 Jan), Rangpanchami (19 Mar), Dussehra Milan (day after Dussehra) and a half day for Ahilya Utsav. **[E]** Free Press Journal, 13 Jan 2025, https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/indore/indore-collector-declares-local-holidays-for-makar-sankranti-rangpanchami-dussehra-more-check-full-list. Diwali 2026 is Sunday 8 November, Dhanteras 6 November; trading communities open new account books (Chopda Pujan) at Diwali, the business new year. **[E, secondary]** https://hindutone.com/festivals/chopda-puja-2026-complete-business-puja-guide-for-diwali/. Coming dates (verify against a panchang): Makar Sankranti 14/15 Jan 2027, Eid ul-Fitr about 10 Mar 2027, Holi 21/22 Mar 2027 with Rangpanchami five days later, Ganesh Chaturthi about 4 Sep 2027. **[E, aggregators]** https://calendarific.com/holidays/2027/IN. Samvatsari, closing Paryushan, is when Jains exchange "Michhami Dukkadam". **[E]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micchami_Dukkadam. Gifting vendors list Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas and New Year as the standard set. **[E, vendor]** https://www.chococraft.in/blogs/corporate-diwali-gifts/corporate-festival-gifting-calendar-2026

Recommendation **[I]**: Diwali is the one greeting every contact expects, personal, on Dhanteras or Diwali day, text not image. Holi, Makar Sankranti, Rangpanchami and Dussehra go to Gold and Platinum only. Michhami Dukkadam to Jain contacts, Eid Mubarak to Muslim contacts and Gurpurab to Sikh contacts land well because they are specific; the same greeting sent to all is noise. Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, national days, "good morning" images and Christmas (unless observed) are noise. No survey ranks greetings by annoyance in India; the 96% daily-spam figure is the nearest evidence that forwards are filed with spam. **[gap]**

**Birthdays.** The lowest-cost, highest-signal touch in Ferrazzi and in every personal CRM. **[E]** Send only if the date came from the person or their public profile; a short text, a call for Gold, never a template image. **[I]**

**LinkedIn milestones.** Congratulate promotions, new roles, awards and five-year-plus anniversaries; skip one- and two-year anniversaries unless the relationship is active; add a line on why the move makes sense. Claimed uplifts such as "27% higher reply rate" are vendor figures without method. **[E, vendor]** https://www.themuse.com/advice/the-tiny-tweak-thatll-make-it-more-likely-that-your-linkedin-messages-get-a-response

**Relevant article.** Law-firm and coaching sources treat an article sent "when it reminds you of someone" as the standard low-pressure touch. **[E, practitioner]** https://natlawreview.com/article/how-to-stay-connected-referral-sources. It reads as care when it is about their business and carries one sentence saying why. **[I]**

**Introductions and referrals back.** BNI's Givers Gain and Grant's giver research agree: an introduction is the strongest Gold touch, and the referrer must be told the outcome. **[E]** https://www.bni.com/?p=64450 ; https://natlawreview.com/article/mastering-referral-relationships-strategies-lawyers

**Voice notes.** Common in Indian workplaces; keep under a minute, record somewhere quiet, and send only to people who accept them; one guide lists voice notes "to people who have not agreed to receive them" among the main professional mistakes. **[E]** https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/lifestyle/whatsapp-voice-message-six-etiquettes-you-should-follow-to-send-voice-message-like-a-pro ; https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/workplace-english/whatsapp/

**Visits and calls.** BNI's weekly one-to-one is the benchmark for Gold; a short call beats three messages for an owner who runs the business from the phone. **[I]** No India-specific evidence found.

**Gifts.** Gifts from non-relatives become taxable for the recipient once the year's aggregate exceeds Rs 50,000 (Section 56(2)(x)); gifts to public officials engage the Prevention of Corruption Act. **[E]** https://www.spotonz.com/blog/gift-tax-section-56-2-x-india/ ; https://www.anti-corruption.com/21298131/diwali-and-good-governance-ethical-giftgiving-in-india.thtml. No benchmark for a normal corporate gift value was found. **[gap]** Vendor etiquette: avoid alcohol, leather and non-vegetarian items unless certain, avoid black packaging and heavy logos, confirm receipt. **[E, vendor]** https://www.chococraft.in/blogs/corporate-diwali-gifts/the-dos-and-donts-of-sending-diwali-gifts-to-business-clients. Rule: Diwali sweets or dry fruit to Gold only; nothing to a Platinum prospect before a first engagement, since it reads as inducement. **[I]**

**Promotion versus care [I].** A touch reads as promotion when it is about the sender, arrives in bulk, asks for a meeting or carries a logo. It reads as care when it is specific to the receiver, needs no reply, and could only come from someone paying attention. Own content should be a minority: Kitces's best-performing mix was majority standardised content, but to paying clients who had opted in. For a prospect network, one own-content item in four or five touches, sent individually with a reason, is the ceiling; a broadcast of own content is not a touch.

## 3. Personal CRM practice in 2026: feature patterns

Sources (all vendor): Dex's 60-app comparison, updated 7 Jul 2026, https://getdex.com/blog/personal-crm-list/ ; lemlist's comparison, updated 7 Oct 2026, https://www.lemlist.com/blog/personal-crm ; Covve App Store listing, https://apps.apple.com/app/id958935377 ; Mesh (formerly Clay), https://me.sh/atlas. **[E]**

**Data kept per person.** Name, phone, email, company and role (auto-filled by folk, Mesh, Dex); source and how met (Covve's card scan; not a named field elsewhere); tags or groups used as tiers (Dex, Mesh, Covve, Notion); "last contacted" and days since (Notion and Airtable templates; auto-logged calls and messages in Covve); an interaction timeline and notes (Mesh, Dex, Monica); family, pets, gifts and debts (Monica); birthdays (Monica, Mesh feed); who-knows-who links (Dex).

**How they remind.** A per-contact keep-in-touch cadence that surfaces the overdue (Dex, Mesh, Monica, Covve); job-change and news alerts (Dex extension, Mesh feed, Covve); weekly digests (Covve). Monica and Notion need manual upkeep.

**What matters, what is bloat [I].** Essential: tier; last-touch date; next-touch date computed from cadence; free-text notes with dates; an overdue list; a record of how the number was obtained (for DPDP). Bloat for one person: pipelines and deal stages, team permissions, dashboards, sequence sending, AI "conversation starters". The Dex comparison itself says sales CRMs are too complex for individuals.

## 4. Interests from social media: acceptable, creepy, unlawful

**Acceptable and effective [I].** Reading a contact's own LinkedIn posts, company announcements, press mentions and association newsletters; noting what they said in person or on a GAINS form. **Creepy:** citing a family photograph or personal Instagram, anything found by searching the person rather than following them, or messaging within minutes of a post.

**LinkedIn User Agreement (3 Nov 2025).** Members must not "develop, support or use software, devices, scripts, robots or any other means or processes" to scrape or copy profiles, must not "use bots or other unauthorized automated methods", and must not "sell or transfer the personal data of others by transferring your connections". **[E]** https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement. Manual reading and notes are unrestricted; bulk export into an enriching tool is where risk begins; hiQ confirmed scraping breaches the agreement. **[E]** https://blog.linkedin.com/2022/november/4/update-hi-q-legal-proceeding

**DPDP Act 2023 and a private CRM [E, not legal advice].** The Act excludes personal data "made or caused to be made publicly available by the Data Principal", with a statutory illustration of data a person posts publicly on social media; the test is how it became public, and the source should be documented. **[E]** India Briefing, 10 Sep 2026, https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-act-publicly-available-personal-data-46899.html/. The personal-and-domestic exemption does not cover a business's CRM. **[E]** https://tsaaro.com/blogs/regulatory-carve-outs-examining-exemptions-under-the-dpdp-act-2023-and-dpdp-rules-2025. Notice-and-consent duties apply from May 2027. **[E]** https://yourstory.com/2025/11/digital-personal-data-protection-dpdp-rules-2025-rollout-over-18-months. Reading **[I]**: a card exchanged at BNI supports follow-up about that acquaintance; a birthday the person told you or a public role is low risk; a spouse's name, health or religion learned from third parties is not. Keep the CRM private, note the source, delete on request.

**WhatsApp terms.** The Business Messaging Policy (updated 23 Sep 2026) governs the Business app and the Platform: contact people only if "they have given you their mobile phone number" and you hold "opt-in permission... confirming that they wish to receive subsequent messages"; do not "spam, or surprise people"; "respect all requests... to block, discontinue, or otherwise opt out", including removing them from your contacts. **[E]** https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/. The consumer Terms bar "bulk messaging, auto-messaging" and "non-personal use". **[E]** https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/terms-of-service. A personal message to someone who gave you their number is within both; a scheduled broadcast to people who did not ask is not. **[I]**

## 5. Scripts and templates

The "no reply needed" pattern closes with a line that removes the obligation to answer; it is the mark of a non-sales message. Vendor follow-up guides say the same for contacts who have not replied: send sparingly, remove pressure. **[E, vendor]** https://www.wati.io/en/blog/whatsapp-follow-up-messages/

**Platinum**
- Link: "Good morning Mr Jain. This piece on dispatch delays in packaging units reminded me of what you said at the AIMP meeting about month-end. Sharing in case it is useful. No reply needed. Regards, Palak."
- Milestone: "Congratulations on the new Pithampur unit, Mr Agrawal. A second plant usually brings the planning questions forward; I hope the transition goes smoothly."
- Own content (one in five): "I wrote a short note on how a 40-person firm can run its follow-ups without the owner chasing. Sending it because it draws on a problem you described. No action needed."
- Diwali: "Wishing you and your family a prosperous Diwali and a good new year of accounts."

**Gold**
- After a referral: "Thank you for introducing Mr Mehta. We met on Tuesday; I will keep you informed of how it proceeds." Later: "Closing the loop: the engagement with Mr Mehta has started. I am grateful for the introduction."
- Give first: "A client of mine in Dewas is looking for a reliable logistics partner. May I share your number with him?"
- Birthday: a call, or "Many happy returns of the day, Sir. I hope the year ahead is a good one for you and the firm."

**Silver**
- Quarterly: "It has been a few months since we met at YEF. I hope the season has been good to you. If you are ever near Vijay Nagar, I would be glad to meet for tea. No reply needed."
- Dormant revival: "It has been over a year since we spoke, which is my fault. I thought of you when reading about the new plastics regulation. I hope all is well."

**Bronze:** Diwali only: "Wishing you a happy and prosperous Diwali, Mr Verma." Sent individually.

**Do not contact:** nothing; record the date and reason.

## 6. Fifteen minutes a week

Vendor practice converges on a fixed Monday slot: review the overdue, act on two or three priority contacts, log it; "a consistent weekly routine of fifteen minutes beats an ambitious system you abandon after three weeks". **[E, vendor]** Dex, 7 Apr 2026, https://getdex.com/blog/personal-crm-networking-guide/

Routine **[I]**: (1) minutes 0 to 3, open the overdue list sorted Platinum, Gold, Silver, plus this week's birthdays and festivals; (2) minutes 3 to 10, send up to five individually written touches, at most one own-content item, and book one Gold one-to-one or call; (3) minutes 10 to 13, log each touch, set the next-touch date, add one line learned; (4) minutes 13 to 15, re-tier at most two people and record any opt-out. After BNI, AIMP, Plast Pack or YEF meetings, enter new cards the same day with source, date and what was discussed.

| Do not contact | Opted out or unsuitable | None | None | 0 | None | None | Keep name, date and reason so they are never re-added |

**Global rules.** No broadcast lists; every message one-to-one, by name, as text. Any "stop", block or silence after a direct question is an opt-out: move to Do-not-contact the same day. Own content is never the first touch after a tier change. Gifts only to Gold, below a value the recipient would need to declare, never to officials.

**Fields per person.** Name; company and role; tier; source (chapter, association, CA, event) and date met; how the number was obtained and any consent (date, words); phone and preferred channel; community or festivals observed (only if volunteered or public); birthday with source; interests and business concerns in their words; family details volunteered; last-touch date, type, channel; next-touch date; touches since last reply (decay counter); referrals given and received with dates; dated notes; opt-out date and reason.

**Evidence gaps.** No study ties touch frequency to referral volume in professional services; no Indian survey ranks festival greetings by annoyance; no benchmark for corporate gift value; Dream 100 cadence is not in a primary source consulted; BNI's "every 6 months" line was seen only in a search summary and should be checked on bni.com.
