# Instagram Feed, Stories and Repurposing: Evidence Note for Venture Drafters

Research date: 29 October 2026. This note builds on `Marketing/_research/content-strategy-2026-10/research-linkedin-instagram.md` (23 October 2026, Part 2), which already covers Mosseri's three ranking signals (watch time, likes, sends), the five-hashtag cap (December 2025), the 3:4 grid and 4:5 upload size (January 2025), the Edits app launch, Trial Reels, the April 2026 extension of the originality policy to photos and carousels, and the 2 to 3 posts a week finding from Dash Social. Those points are not repeated; this note cites only what is newer or more specific.

Labels: **[E]** a published source states it (URL and date given); **[I]** my inference. Nearly all data comes from analytics vendors and trade press; Meta publishes no ranking weights. Where evidence does not exist, the note says so.

---

## 1. Reels, carousels and single images

**Reach.** [E] Metricool's 2026 study (24.4M posts, 375k accounts, Jan–Feb 2025 vs Jan–Feb 2026, published 16 June 2026) finds Reels earn more than four times the interactions of single images, average Reel watch time rose to 8.5 seconds (more than doubling year on year), and single-image reach fell 21.96%, interactions 25.41% and engagement rate 45.98%. https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/ . Secondary coverage adds that Reels reach 29% more people than carousels; that figure is not in the press release and is unconfirmed. [E] Socialinsider's 2026 benchmarks put reach rate slightly in favour of carousels (4.50% vs 4.10% for Reels); the two largest datasets disagree on the reach gap but agree single images trail. https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram

**Saves and sends.** [E] Carousels generate nine times the saves of single images (Metricool, above). [E] Socialinsider's median figures are narrower: 37 saves for carousels, 35 for Reels, 10 for images; Reels lead on shares (5 vs 3) and comments (33 vs 25) (via https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/reels-vs-carousel-instagram-formats , 2026). [I] The division of labour is consistent across sources: Reels for reach to non-followers and for sends; carousels for saves and engagement per person reached; single images for neither.

**Profile visits and DMs.** No evidence found. The one claim that carousels draw three times the profile visits of Reels (rescroll.ai) has no disclosed method. No study isolates small B2B accounts. [I] Measure profile visits and follows per post in Insights over the first 12 weeks rather than adopt a benchmark.

**Reel length.** [E] Socialinsider analysed 9.77M organic Reels from brands with 1k–1M followers (Jan–Jun 2026; published 14 July 2026, updated 20 October 2026). Engagement rate by length: 0–30s 0.28%; 30–45s 0.30%; 45–60s 0.35% (highest); 60–90s 0.30%; 120–180s 0.33%; over 180s 0.15%. Median views peak at 45–60s (10,374) against 4,700 under 30s and 4,428 over 180s. The same article states that Reels longer than 3 minutes are not placed in the Reels tab or recommended to non-followers. https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/ . [E] CreatorHouse's self-run test of 50 Reels (April 2026) favoured 12–18 seconds, with a B2B cohort at 20–25 seconds; the sample is too small to override Socialinsider. https://creatorhouse.app/blog/instagram-reel-length-2026-test . [I] For this audience, 35 to 60 seconds is the defensible default: one complete idea, inside the bracket where views and engagement both peak. Reels can be recorded up to 20 minutes, but anything over 3 minutes loses recommendation [E, Socialinsider above].

**Talking-head vs animation vs b-roll with captions.** No evidence found; no 2026 study isolates presentation style, and vendor claims that burned-in captions lift retention by 15–25% are unsourced. [I] Test the three styles with one script through Trial Reels and judge by skip rate and average watch time.

## 2. Stories

**What belongs where.** [E] Instagram ranks Stories by the viewer's likelihood to tap, like or reply, and by closeness; Mosseri said Stories are built for existing connections and that Feed is the better route to wide reach (Social Media Today, 28 January 2025). https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-stories-ranking-factors-2025/738541/ . [E] On 6 April 2026 Mosseri said resharing a feed post to Stories does not meaningfully raise reach, and that reach "hacks" usually do not work or are shut down; Stories scheduling is under internal debate but not confirmed. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-debunks-popular-engagement-hack/816781/ . [I] For a professional-services brand: feed and Reels carry teaching assets and proof; Stories carry the working day (site visits, a system going live, a question to followers) and exist to start DM conversations, not to win followers.

**Cadence.** [E] Dash Social's benchmark (2,000+ brands with 1k+ followers, Jan–Jun 2025, updated 19 December 2025) reports brands post about two Stories a day across all size tiers, with a 70% completion rate and 5% exit rate; small accounts (up to 190k followers) complete at 68.3% and exit at 6.0%. https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/every-instagram-stories-performance-benchmark-you-need-to-know . [E] Rival IQ's older report (data to November 2023) found retention falls as frame count rises. [I] Two to four frames on three to five days a week is realistic for one founder; a day with nothing to show should have no Story.

**Stickers and DMs.** [E] Vendor compilations put poll, question and slider interaction at 12–18% of viewers (LeadResponse, 2026) or 15–25% (Upgrow, 2026); Outfame reports a 41% rise in DM volume on its own users' accounts after adopting stickers. None is independently verified. https://leadresponse.co/blog/instagram-engagement-statistics ; https://www.upgrow.com/blog/instagram-stories-2026-completion-rates-views-engagement-benchmarks ; https://www.outfame.com/blog/instagram-stories-statistics . [E] Replies to a Story are a ranking signal for the next one (Social Media Today, January 2025, above). [I] Only the question sticker produces a private, answerable message; use it weekly with a specific operational question. Polls raise taps, not conversations.

**Highlights.** No benchmark data found. [I] Highlights act as a profile menu; a sensible set is Case studies, How we work, Systems, About Palak, Clients speak.

## 3. Repurposing long YouTube video into Reels and Shorts

**What works.** [E] Shorts can run to 3 minutes (since 15 October 2024) and must be square or vertical; Piktochart's analysis of 5,400 Shorts found 30–60 seconds performed best. https://piktochart.com/blog/how-long-youtube-shorts/ . [E] Podcast-clip guidance converges on 30–55 seconds, opening on the strongest line with no preamble, and a text overlay or jump cut to skip set-up (FrameOS, 2026). https://frameos.studio/blog/how-to-make-shorts-from-podcasts . [E] Vendors place the skip decision within one to three seconds; the percentages are unverified. [I] A repurposed clip should run 35–60 seconds, open on the claim or the number, carry burned-in captions, and be reframed to 9:16 with the speaker centred.

**What fails.** [E] A Trial Reel may get limited reach if the same content was shared before, and an identical re-upload as a trial is downranked (EngageDM, 30 September 2025, citing Mosseri and the Help Center; https://engagedm.co.uk/2025/09/30/trial-reels-rules/ ). [E] Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to non-followers (Socialinsider, above). [E] Reels cannot be cross-posted natively to Threads; Social Media Today recommends posting to each app individually. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-activates-threads-crossposting-facebook-instagram/725058/ . [I] A clip that assumes the viewer knows the case study, or opens with the founder introducing herself, will be skipped.

**Repurposed vs native share.** No evidence found on a ratio. [E] The April 2026 originality policy penalises reposting others' content; clips cut from one's own long-form video are original (Tubefilter, 30 April 2026, https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/ ). [I] A 30 to 60 minute case study yields four to six usable clips, a month of weekly Reels. Cap repurposed clips at half of Reels so the account also shows native, present-tense material.

**Tools.** [E] Edits (Meta, free, iOS and Android) offers multilingual auto-captions, a teleprompter, cutouts, green screen and Insights (TechCrunch, 12 August 2025, https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/12/a-guide-to-using-edits-metas-new-capcut-rival-for-short-form-video-editing ); the desktop version announced in June 2026 is live only for selected creators as of 25 October 2026 (Social Media Today, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/edits-comes-to-iphone-duo-and-desktop/833909/ ). Clip-selection tools in common use: OpusClip (free tier; Starter about USD 15 a month), Klap, Vizard, CapCut; Descript for text-based editing. No 2026 head-to-head test of clip-selection quality was found. [I] OpusClip or Descript for first-pass selection and reframing; Edits for captions and polish.

## 4. Company page vs founder's personal account

[E] Collab posts admit up to five collaborators and appear to the followers of every collaborator; collaborators cannot be added to Stories. Mosseri has said collab posts tend to reach more people when the larger account creates the post (Buffer, 2 July 2025, https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-collab-post/ ). [E] Emplifi (H1 2026, 520k posts, 3,791 profiles, published 15 September 2026) found accepted collabs (partner publishes, brand is invited) deliver the largest uplift; for brands under 10,000 followers, accepted collab posts earned about five times the interactions of non-collab posts, and Reels showed the strongest collab gains. https://emplifi.io/resources/instagram-collaborations-boost-organic-engagement/ . [E] No study compares a founder's personal account with a company page on Instagram; the LinkedIn evidence in the earlier note does not transfer.

[I] Rules that follow: Palak's personal account publishes every Reel and carousel and invites the company page as collaborator, so one post sits on both grids without a duplicate. The company page publishes alone only static trust material (client logos, services, testimonials). Never post the same file separately on both accounts. Stories stay on the personal account. Cross-posting to Facebook suits an Indian SME audience (the earlier note records Facebook as the top social app among MSMEs), but post Reels natively to each app rather than through automatic sharing.

## 5. Days and times in IST

[E] Sprout Social (about 2 billion engagements, 307k profiles, 27 November 2025 to 27 February 2026, published 31 March 2026, all times local to the audience): Instagram peaks Tuesday 1–7 pm, Wednesday 12–9 pm, Monday 2–4 pm, Thursday 12–2 pm; weekends weakest. Software and Technology, the nearest B2B category, peaks weekdays 11 am–3 pm. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/ . [E] India-specific guides (WSCube Tech, MyDigitalSevak, Sociolyzer, 2026) cluster on 7–9 pm IST, Tuesday to Thursday, but none publishes a sample or method; evidence quality is low. [I] Two candidate slots: 12–2 pm IST (matches Sprout's global and B2B windows) and 7–9 pm IST (after the shop or plant closes; the Indian consensus). Hold one for 8 weeks, switch, compare in Insights. Frequency stays at two feed posts a week (one Reel, one carousel) as in the earlier note, plus one Trial Reel a week when a clip batch exists.

## 6. What is new in 2026 and what it changes

- **Originality extended to photos and carousels (30 April 2026)** [E] Accounts that mostly repost others' photos, carousels or Reels without meaningful change lose recommendation to non-followers; followers still see them. Screenshots with credit and speed changes do not count as original (Tubefilter, above). Effect: never repost client or partner graphics; use collab or tag.
- **Reposts feature (6 August 2025)** [E] Users can repost public Reels and feed posts to a Reposts tab, with credit (PetaPixel, https://petapixel.com/2025/08/06/instagram-adds-a-reposts-tab-to-users-profiles-similar-to-tiktok/ ). Effect: a repost by a client or CA is a send-like signal; ask for it.
- **AI-generated profile label (31 August 2026)** [E] Profiles featuring an AI-generated person without the label have reach reduced; AI used to edit photos, polish captions or make graphics needs no label (TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/ ). Effect: none for a real founder; no AI avatar as presenter.
- **Insights redesign (April 2026)** [E, secondary only] Three tabs and new metrics: skip rate, share rate, views over time (Inro, https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-insights-update ). No primary announcement found. Effect: skip rate is the hook metric; share rate is the sends metric.
- **Trial Reels** [E] Scheduling added early 2026; identical re-uploads get limited reach; a daily cap of about five is reported but unverified (EngageDM, above). Effect: trials test new hooks or styles, never a second run of the same file.
- **Edits desktop** [E] Limited release as of 25 October 2026 (above). Effect: none yet.
- **Reels to 20 minutes** [E] Recording limit only; recommendation stops at 3 minutes (Socialinsider, above). Effect: the long case study stays on YouTube.
- **DM features** No 2026 change found that affects a small professional account beyond Meta Business Suite's free instant replies, keyword replies and comment-to-DM private replies (one message per comment, within seven days) [E, vendor sources].
