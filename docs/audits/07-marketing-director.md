## The content, judged against the plan

- **Mix and cadence.** The December Reel slot on the 28th now carries a science piece; December's Mondays run problem, problem, problem, science: within the plan's "about a third" for science. The piece is the designer's explanation in plain words (work with no visible state), which is the part no rival says.
- **ICP.** The late delivery found out from the client is the second of the five pains the brief names. "The step you could not see is where it stopped" is the owner's experience, not ours.
- **Storyline.** The solution rows name a stage, an owner and a date on the owner's own sheet, and the owner's screen; the honest limit ("Your team still does the work") is in the script. The frame is in the piece.
- **Register and channel.** First row on camera with the same words on screen, cutaways marked, 40 to 55 seconds at 140 words a minute, caption in four short sentences, hashtags in the list. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no claim.
- **Across the library.** The review pass (written by hand tonight, see 09-content-icp-register.md) is what carries the frame into the 85 imported pieces; the director's own walk could not do that piece by piece.

## Verdict before the rework

The plan's path runs: a moment to a placed, approved piece with its cover in under fifteen minutes with the runner answering. Two defaults would make the director stumble every time: the piece type on "Propose" and "New piece", and the card template for video pieces.

## What was fixed

1. "Propose from a moment" and "New piece" default to "B-roll video", the type a new video is written as; the animated types stay in the list for the imported pieces.
2. Pictures opens on the hook card (the Reel cover, portrait for the feed) for b-roll, animated reels, episodes and Shorts; the Pictures card on the piece says so.
3. The Instagram preview says "Video not filmed yet; the script is below" when a video piece has no file, so a review card reads as work in progress rather than a blank.

## What was left

- "Propose from a moment" does not know which slot the director has in mind; the piece is placed afterwards from the calendar. Carrying a slot into Propose is a follow-up.
- December's pillar mix is read off the calendar by eye; a pillar count per month on the calendar is a follow-up Palak may want.
